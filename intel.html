Objection Handling

"I already use a warm-up tool"

Most tools use shared networks — your warm-up pool is shared with competitors. Inboxy's network is private and isolated.

"Too expensive"

$47 flat vs $279+/mo for Warmy. One extra meeting booked pays for 6 months.

"I handle deliverability manually"

Manual warm-up doesn't adapt in real time. Inboxy's AI pauses and adjusts automatically if spam signals emerge.

"Is AI safe for this?"

Inboxy follows email best practices — consistent volumes, whitehat content, self-regulates if ISP flags appear.
